# **Pricing Updates: Tile API Adjustment & Tracking SDK Cost Reduction**

At **Gebeta**, we continuously evaluate our infrastructure, performance, and cost structure to ensure we deliver reliable, scalable, and fairly priced location services. As part of this effort, we’re introducing two important pricing updates that affect our **Tile API** and **Tracking SDK**.

---

## 📈 Tile API Price Increase

Over the past months, usage of our Tile API has grown significantly, driven by higher map loads, increased zoom-level requests, and expanded geographic coverage.

To continue providing:
- High availability and low latency
- Improved tile generation and caching
- Ongoing investments in rendering performance and reliability

we are adjusting the pricing of the **Tile API**. This change ensures the service remains sustainable while allowing us to keep improving map quality and performance for high-traffic and production-grade applications.

---

## 📉 Tracking SDK Price Decrease (Now More Affordable)

We’re also happy to announce a **price decrease for our Tracking SDK**.

With the introduction of **HTTP-based tracking**, we’ve significantly optimized how tracking data is collected and processed. This new approach reduces system overhead while maintaining accuracy and reliability.

Key benefits include:
- Lower infrastructure and processing costs
- Reduced client-side complexity
- Improved scalability for high-frequency and fleet-level tracking

Thanks to these efficiencies, we’re passing the savings directly to you by lowering the Tracking SDK price.
